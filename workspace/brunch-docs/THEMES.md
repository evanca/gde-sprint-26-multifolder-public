# Brunch Themes

The Brunch Club RSVP Kit currently offers the following themes. Each theme is
defined in `brunch-content-data/themes.json` and validated against
`brunch-shared-schema/brunch-theme.schema.json`. Each theme's **accent** is an
[Open Color](../open-color/) token (external, read-only) applied by the frontend.

| Theme | Tagline | Host | Seats | Accent |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Garden Citrus Table | Sun-warmed mornings with grapefruit and herbs. | Mara Lindqvist | 12 | `--oc-orange-3` |
| Cozy Cocoa Corner | Slow brunch with deep cocoa and warm spice. | Daniel Okafor | 8 | `--oc-orange-9` |
| Harbor Greens Brunch | Crisp, briny plates by the water. | Sofia Reyes | 16 | `--oc-teal-4` |

## Garden Citrus Table

- **Menu:** Grapefruit and mint salad; Ricotta toast with thyme honey; Blood orange spritz.
- **Accent:** `--oc-orange-3`

## Cozy Cocoa Corner

- **Menu:** Dark cocoa banana bread; Cinnamon oat porridge; Spiced mocha.
- **Accent:** `--oc-orange-9`

## Harbor Greens Brunch

- **Menu:** Cucumber dill tartine; Smoked trout bowl; Sparkling sea-salt lemonade.
- **Accent:** `--oc-teal-4`
